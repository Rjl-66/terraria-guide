This means the page will walk you through a specific task, strategy, or enemy/boss fight.
This guide was last updated for version 2.1.2.
This guide is not updated for the current version of the mod. Some or all of its advice may not be applicable to the current patch.

This guide is intended to list viable options for players depending on their progression stage and the class they've chosen. Think of this guide as a menu — there are many options to choose from, and you should try out different things to see which work best for you. Additionally, this guide is a starting point for you to build your own loadout from; you are encouraged to make changes to your liking. Be sure to check the individual pages for the items to understand how to craft them or their functionalities.

You should note the following things:

  • This guide is written assuming you are playing on Expert difficulty and Revengeance Mode. This guide may not be accurate for Death Mode, or anything on Master difficulty.
  • Calamity changes many vanilla items and mechanics. You can read these changes here.
  • Summoner weapons and minions will only do 0.75x damage if used alongside weapons from a different class.
  • The rogue class is a new class created by the Calamity Mod. It has a unique stealth mechanic that adds new attacks to its weapons. Not using stealth and simply using the weapon as fast as possible is referred to as "spam".
  • Each of the entries are sorted by category and listed alphabetically. The order does not signify how powerful something is.
  • Each of the items listed here is good, so if you don't like one, try another!

Using the guide[edit]

This guide uses the following symbols to help you understand how to use some items:

† — This item can be difficult or risky to use, and requires boss preparation

C — This item is best used for crowd control, such as Events or Worm bosses

+ — Support items that debuff the enemy or benefit the player in some way, or secondary weapons that can be used occasionally

≤ — Or variants / upgrades

Δ — This item has significant Calamity changes by way of functionality

* — This item might be difficult and tedious to get at this stage

Ω — These items should be used together

Items restricted to Revengeance Mode or higher will be italicized.

Pre-Eater of Worlds/Brain of Cthulhu [edit]

Defeating your first bosses vastly expands your options: After the Eye of Cthulhu is defeated, you should explore the Underground Jungle, fight the Blood Moon and Goblin Army, and challenge the newly unlocked Acid Rain event. Defeating the Desert Scourge unlocks the Giant Clam miniboss and is the point where you should explore the Sunken Sea. Crabulon can also be fought for weapons and the Mushroom Plasma Root power-up. Lastly, maxing out your health and mana will help you prepare for your first major step in progression.

Pre-Wall of Flesh [edit]

With Skeletron defeated, you should explore the dungeon then head to the Abyss and loot its newly unlocked Ancient Treasure Chests. Down in the Underworld, Shadow Chests can now be opened, and you should now explore the Brimstone Crags. Once ready, you can fight The Slime God, who is an optional boss which drops useful items as well as the Electrolyte Gel Pack power-up. Additionally, you can now use Blood Orbs at an Alchemy Table to make most available potions, including some traditionally Hardmode-exclusive ones, such as Lifeforce Potions. When you are prepared, defeat the Wall of Flesh and enter Hardmode.
