The Raider's Talisman is a craftable Pre-Hardmode rogue accessory. When equipped, if the player performs a stealth strike, non-stealth strike rogue hits will gain a temporary 15% bonus to their critical strike chance that lasts 10 seconds.

History

edit
These history sections are still a work-in-progress, and may not yet contain changes relevant to the current version of the Calamity Mod.
  • 2.2.2: Now uses 5 Flinx Fur in its recipe.
  • 2.2.0: Fixed the critical strike chance boost applying at the wrong times and improperly being able to apply to sub-projectiles from stealth strikes.
  • 2.1.0: Buffed critical strike chance boost from 12% to 15%, boost now lasts 10 seconds instead of 5, and it no longer decays over time.
  • 2.0.1.003: Added new stealth strike sounds.
  • 2.0.1.002: Now increases non-stealth strike rogue critical strike chance by 12% after performing a stealth strike which decays over 5 seconds, instead of gradually increasing rogue damage after landing critical strikes which lasts until death.
  • 1.5.1.001: Resprited. Previous sprite was
  • 1.4.4.001: Added lines to its tooltip saying the maximum stack and damage boost, along with a dynamically updating line showing the current stack level.
  • 1.4.3.002: Critical strike effect now stacks to 150 for a total damage boost of 15% instead of stacking to 250 with a total damage boost of 25%.
  • 1.4.3.001: Added a cooldown of 0.5 seconds for the damage stacking effect.
  • 1.4.2.108: Tooltip will now list the player's current stacked crit boost.
  • 1.4.2.004: Buffed maximum damage boost from 15% to 25%.
  • 1.4.1.003: Fixed tooltip saying that rogue weapons boost throwing damage instead of rogue damage.
  • 1.4.1.001: Updated tooltip to reflect throwing being reworked into rogue.
  • 1.4.0.001: Increased the amount of times the effect can stack from 50 to 250.
  • 1.3.2.001:
    • Reworked:
      • Now provides its buffs when a throwing weapon lands a critical strike on an enemy instead of when the wearer is damaged.
      • The effect now only stacks up to 50 times instead of 100, for a maximum throwing boost of 15% instead of 40%.
      • Removed extra damage taken effect.
  • 1.2.3.004: Lowered the extra damage inflicted from 25 to 10.
  • 1.2.0.001: Introduced.
